Good morning everyone, this is the Q2 business review, let's go through the agenda quickly because we only have the room for an hour. Starting with revenue. Second quarter revenue was 3.75 million euros, up 8 percent year over year. Enterprise was flat, most renewals are concentrated in the third quarter so that is expected. The small business segment actually grew by 2 percent, helped by the spring promotion, and it was the fastest growing segment this quarter. Gross margin came in at 69 percent, slightly down because of the one-off cost of the new support tooling. Finance flagged that the price increase planned for July could put pressure on small business renewals, so they will monitor that closely. On hiring, we closed four data engineering roles and two account executives this quarter. Time to hire went down to 38 days on average. We renewed the contract with the TalentBridge recruiting agency for another year, because they filled most of the senior roles and our own recruiters are fully booked. There is no hiring freeze planned at the moment, but finance asked every department to re-justify open roles in September. Next, infrastructure. We confirmed the decision to exit the Frankfurt colocation facility, the contract ends in December. The migration will start with search, because it has the fewest dependencies, followed by notifications. Nobody has looked at billing in detail yet, the platform team will do an assessment next quarter, and the Oracle license is on the list of things to check. Customer health next. Churn in Asia Pacific was 5 percent, in line with Europe and North America, and net revenue retention in APAC was 104 percent. The Japanese reseller partnership is working well and the reseller handles first-line support for their accounts in Japanese. On security, the penetration test with Castell Security is booked for the third quarter, and the scope covers the analytics pipeline, the support tool and the customer portal. We also finished the single sign-on rollout, every internal tool now uses SSO. Finally, events. The company offsite is planned for March in Lisbon, the venue is booked for three days, and the team is also looking at a new office in Lisbon for customer success. That's the Q2 review, thank you.
